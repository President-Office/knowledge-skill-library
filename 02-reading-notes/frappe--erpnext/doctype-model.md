# ERPNext - DocType 模型

项目主卡：[[frappe--erpnext]]

## 核心关系

[[DocType]] 是 ERPNext 的核心建模单位。它把字段、权限、表单、控制器和业务事件组织到同一个元数据模型里。

- `erpnext/accounts/doctype`
- `erpnext/stock/doctype`
- `erpnext/selling/doctype`
- `erpnext/buying/doctype`
